AI Accessibility & Compliance Workflow

Turning a fragmented regulated workflow into an AI-assisted product concept with grounded outputs, explicit evaluation, and human review.

The problem

Accessibility and compliance review can span issue detection, interpretation, remediation guidance, evidence gathering, and expert verification. The product question was not simply “Can AI find issues?” It was: where can AI accelerate the workflow without creating unacceptable trust or compliance risk?

What I owned

  • Problem framing and workflow decomposition
  • Product requirements and success metrics
  • AI/tool boundaries and human-review strategy
  • MVP architecture and hands-on prototyping
  • Evaluation dimensions and production-readiness criteria

Product workflow

Input / UI→Detection→Retrieval + Tools→AI Interpretation→Remediation + Evidence→Human Review

The workflow separates deterministic or tool-based checks from AI interpretation and expert review. This makes failure modes easier to observe and creates explicit control points.

The key product decision

The goal was not maximum automation. In a regulated workflow, a plausible but unsupported answer can be worse than no answer.

ApproachAdvantageRisk
Fully automated AIFastest end-to-end workflowHigher false-positive and trust risk
Rules / deterministic checks onlyPredictable and explainableLimited interpretation and remediation support
Hybrid workflow ✓Automation where useful + explicit control pointsMore orchestration complexity
DecisionUse a hybrid workflow: deterministic/tool-based checks where appropriate, AI-assisted interpretation and remediation, evidence grounding, and human review for higher-risk decisions.

AI evaluation framework

DimensionWhat I would measure
Detection qualityPrecision / recall by issue type where ground truth is available
GroundednessWhether findings and recommendations are supported by retrieved evidence
False positivesIncorrect flags per review and by category
Remediation usefulnessReviewer acceptance / usefulness of recommendations
Human escalationShare and type of cases requiring expert review
PerformanceLatency across the end-to-end workflow
EconomicsModel/tool cost per completed review

Current stage

This work is an independently developed 0→1 product concept and MVP prototype. It is not presented here as a customer-deployed or revenue-generating product. The value of the case study is the product reasoning, architecture, prototyping, and evaluation approach.

What I learned / what I would do next

Build the evaluation dataset earlier.A compelling prototype can prove feasibility, but a representative eval set is needed to distinguish a good demo from a product that can be trusted. My next step would be to create a labeled benchmark by issue category and use it to drive iteration and human-review thresholds.
