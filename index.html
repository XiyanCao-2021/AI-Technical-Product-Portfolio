CASE STUDY 01 • 0→1 APPLIED AI

AI Accessibility & Compliance Workflow

Designed and prototyped an AI-assisted workflow for regulated software teams, with explicit evaluation and human review.

0→1AI ProductRAGEvaluationHITL

Problem

Accessibility review spans detection, remediation guidance, evidence generation, and expert verification. The challenge was to accelerate the workflow without sacrificing trust.

My role

Problem framing, workflow design, requirements, architecture, MVP prototyping, success metrics, and evaluation strategy.

Key decisions

  • Use AI for interpretation and remediation support—not unchecked final decisions.
  • Ground outputs in retrieved evidence and structured checks.
  • Define escalation paths and human-review thresholds.
  • Evaluate groundedness, false positives, usefulness, latency, and cost.

What this proves

0→1 AI product thinking, technical prototyping, responsible AI judgment, and the ability to turn an ambiguous workflow into a testable product.
