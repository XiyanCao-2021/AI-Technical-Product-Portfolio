AI Deployment Readiness & Evaluation Platform

A 0→1 product concept and MVP for helping product and engineering teams determine whether AI features, agents, and workflows are ready for production.

The problem

AI teams can build a feature or agent that looks impressive in a demo but still struggle to answer a harder question: Is it ready for production? Production readiness requires more than model accuracy. Teams need evidence across task success, groundedness, tool behavior, safety, reliability, latency, cost, fallback behavior, and human escalation.

The product opportunity was to make that launch decision more systematic, repeatable, and actionable.

What I owned

  • Defined the 0→1 product concept, target workflow, and readiness criteria.
  • Designed the evaluation journey from test scenarios through deployment decision.
  • Defined evaluation dimensions, quality gates, failure categories, and human-review points.
  • Built an MVP prototype combining LLM evaluation, retrieval, tool integration, and agentic orchestration.
  • Connected evaluation results to remediation recommendations rather than producing scores alone.

End-to-end product workflow

AI Feature / Agent→Test Scenarios→Automated Evals→Failure Analysis→Readiness Score→Remediation→Human Go / No-Go

The product is designed to turn evaluation into a deployment workflow: identify what failed, why it matters, what should change, and whether the system meets the quality bar for its risk level.

The key product decision

A single aggregate “AI score” is easy to understand but can hide critical failure modes. A system might score well overall while still failing on a small set of high-risk tool calls or unsupported answers.

ApproachAdvantageRisk
Single readiness scoreSimple executive signalCan mask severe category-specific failures
Raw evaluation dashboardRich technical detailHarder to translate into a launch decision
Risk-based quality gates + summary score ✓Clear decision signal with failure-level visibilityRequires thoughtful thresholds by use case
DecisionUse category-level quality gates and risk-based thresholds, then summarize them into an overall readiness view. High-risk failures can block deployment even when aggregate performance looks strong.

Current stage

This is an independently developed 0→1 product concept and MVP prototype. It is not presented as a customer-deployed or revenue-generating product. The case study demonstrates product framing, AI evaluation strategy, technical prototyping, and production-readiness thinking.

What I would build next

Move from generic evaluation to use-case-specific readiness profiles.A support agent, research assistant, and action-taking enterprise agent should not share identical launch thresholds. The next product step would let teams define risk profiles, critical scenarios, required quality gates, and release criteria for each AI workflow—then compare results across model, prompt, tool, and version changes.

The portfolio intentionally distinguishes prototype work from production/customer outcomes.
