CASE STUDY 02 • ENTERPRISE / FORWARD DEPLOYED PRODUCT

Enterprise Assessment Platform at Scale

Bridging enterprise customers and engineering to turn high-stakes requirements into scalable platform capabilities and reliable production delivery.

eMetricSenior Technical Product ManagerEnterprise SaaSCloud
ContextMy roleCustomer loopTradeoffOutcomesLearning

Context

The platform supported high-stakes digital assessment workflows for enterprise education customers. Product decisions had to balance customer commitments, compliance, platform strategy, delivery effort, scalability, and production reliability.

What I owned

  • Product strategy and roadmap for a mission-critical enterprise platform
  • Enterprise discovery, onboarding, requirements workshops, and product demos
  • Prioritization and technical/product tradeoffs with engineering and executives
  • Cross-functional response to customer-impacting product and production issues
  • Roadmap feedback loop from recurring customer needs to reusable capabilities

The customer → product → engineering loop

Customer workflow→Discovery / requirements→Solution options→Engineering tradeoffs→Delivery / production→Reusable product learning

A recurring challenge was distinguishing a customer-specific request from a broader platform need. The objective was to solve real customer problems without accumulating unsustainable one-off customization.

A recurring product tradeoff

ChoiceBenefitCost / risk
One-off customizationCan satisfy an immediate customer request quicklyMaintenance burden, fragmentation, future delivery cost
Wait for generic platform capabilityCleaner platformMay miss customer timing or business commitment
Reusable capability with scoped delivery ✓Addresses customer outcome while strengthening the platformRequires disciplined scope and stakeholder alignment
Product principleUse discovery and recurring field signals to identify the underlying workflow need, then prefer reusable platform capabilities when the pattern supports it.

Selected outcomes

2M+Concurrent users supported
99.99%Availability during high-stakes testing windows
$178M+Multi-year enterprise contract supported by strategic platform capabilities

Execution at scale

Directed a 20-person distributed engineering team and led cloud platform modernization / Azure-Kubernetes deployment while balancing reliability, deployment risk, customer commitments, and roadmap delivery.

What I learned

The best customer solution is not always the most customized solution.Forward-deployed product work is strongest when field learning improves the core product. A PM must understand the immediate customer outcome deeply enough to separate the true workflow requirement from the requested implementation.
